The only date on a bag of coffee I care about is the roast date. Not the best before.

Here's why, and how to check your own bag.
---
Coffee is a fresh food, like bread.

You wouldn't buy bread without knowing when it was baked. And a best before date doesn't tell you when coffee was roasted.
---
I went and checked. Last month I bought a bag at the supermarket.

Best before date: yes.
Roast date: nowhere on it.

So I can't tell you how old that coffee is.
---
What to do: turn the bag over and look for the words "roasted on".

If there's a date, count back from today. My rule of thumb is to drink it within about a month of the roast date. My rule, not a law.
---
Is old coffee safe? A customer asked me that last week about a bag roasted 4 months ago.

Yes, it's safe. It just won't taste like much.
---
