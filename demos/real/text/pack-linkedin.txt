Last week a customer asked me if coffee roasted 4 months ago was still OK to drink.

She'd found one of our bags at the back of her cupboard.

I told her yes, it's safe. It just won't taste like much.

Her reply is the bit that got me. She said she had never looked for a roast date before. Not on our coffee, not on any coffee. She didn't know it was a thing.

That date is the reason our business is set up the way it is.

We roast to order, on Mondays and Thursdays, and it's at your door within 7 days of roasting. And the roast date goes on the front of the bag, in big type. Not hidden on the back.

None of that helps much if nobody knows to look.

So I'm curious. Is there a roast date on the coffee in your kitchen right now?
