I think the best before date is the least useful thing printed on a bag of coffee.

The date I want is the day it was roasted. Last month I bought a supermarket bag to check, and it wasn't on there.

I'm not against best before dates. They answer a fair question: is this still OK to drink?

They just don't answer mine: when was this roasted?

You wouldn't choose a loaf of bread by how long it's allowed to sit on the shelf. You'd want to know when it was baked. Coffee is the same kind of food.

Last week a customer found a bag of ours in her cupboard, 4 months after we roasted it, and asked if she could still drink it. She could. It just wasn't going to taste of much. Then she told me she had never looked for a roast date on any coffee.

So ours goes on the front of the bag, in big type. And we roast to order, so it's at your door within 7 days of that date.

Maybe I'm too close to this. If you choose coffee by the best before date and it works for you, tell me what I'm missing.
