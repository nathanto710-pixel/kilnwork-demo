# Two bags, one date

Format: short
Target: 30 seconds
Pace: 150

## Hook
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| This coffee has no roast date. | Detail: a supermarket bag turned slowly in two hands. Text on screen: "No roast date." |

## Beat 1 - I checked
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| I bought it last month to check. Best before date, yes. Roast date, nowhere. | Close up: finger on the best before date, then the bag flipped front, back, bottom. |

## Beat 2 - what that means
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| So I can't tell you how old it is. Nobody holding this bag can. | Mid: Sam at the bench, holds the bag up, then sets it down. |

## Turn
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| Now this one. [pause 1] The date's on the front. | Close up: a Lowtide bag lifted into frame, thumb next to the roast date. |

## Beat 3 - why it matters
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| You wouldn't buy bread without knowing when it was baked. Same thing. | Mid: Sam to camera, both bags on the bench. |

## Call to action
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| Go and look at the bag in your kitchen. | Mid: Sam points past the lens. Text on screen: "Check your bag." |

## Notes
- B-roll to film: the supermarket bag turned in two hands; finger on the best before date; thumb beside the roast date on the Lowtide bag.
- Filmed alone at the roastery bench, phone upright, propped at eye height.
