# Hooks: how to check the roast date

Lowtide Coffee is a fictional brand used as an example. Made by following the hook-lab steps on `input-notes.md`.

## The promise
This piece gives the reader a way to pick up any bag of coffee and tell how old it is, or tell that they cannot.

## The facts list
1. A customer emailed last week about a bag of Lowtide coffee she found at the back of her cupboard. Roast date 4 months old.
2. She asked if it was OK to drink. Sam's answer for that bag: safe, but it will not taste like much.
3. She had never looked for a roast date on any coffee. (Sam's summary, not her exact words.)
4. Last month Sam bought one supermarket bag: best before date, no roast date anywhere.
5. Lowtide's roast date is on the front of the bag in big type.
6. Roast to order, Mondays and Thursdays. At your door within 7 days of roasting.
7. Method: turn the bag over, look for "roasted on", count back. Sam's rule of thumb: within about a month.
8. Sam's own lines: "Coffee is a fresh food, like bread." "You wouldn't buy bread without knowing when it was baked." "The roast date is the only date on the bag I care about."

## Top 3
Five hooks tied on 9 behind the winner. The tie was broken by reading aloud for Sam's voice, and by choosing different patterns so there is a real choice.

### 1. "How old is the coffee in your kitchen?" (10)
- **Why it won:** a stranger understands it at once, it is about their own kitchen, and it is a question they may not be able to answer. The piece answers it in the next step.
- **Weakness:** it is a common shape of hook. It relies on the next line being concrete.
- **Video**
  - SAY: How old is the coffee in your kitchen?
  - SHOW: Detail: a hand takes a bag of coffee from a shelf and turns it over, looking for something.
  - TEXT ON SCREEN: How old is this?
- **Carousel:** `{ "layout": "hook", "title": "How old is your *coffee?*" }`
- **LinkedIn**
  - Line 1: Do you know how old the coffee in your kitchen is?
  - Line 2: A customer of ours found out last week. Her bag was roasted 4 months ago.

### 2. "This bag has a best before date. It has no roast date anywhere." (9)
- **Why it won:** it is proof you can see. On video the bag does half the work, and the missing date is the gap.
- **Weakness:** without the picture it is weaker, and it is 13 words.
- **Video**
  - SAY: This bag has a best before date. It has no roast date anywhere.
  - SHOW: Close up: the supermarket bag turned front, back and bottom in two hands.
  - TEXT ON SCREEN: No roast date.
- **Carousel:** `{ "layout": "hook", "title": "Best before: yes. Roast date: *nowhere.*" }`
- **LinkedIn**
  - Line 1: Last month I bought a bag of coffee at the supermarket to check one thing.
  - Line 2: It had a best before date. It had no roast date anywhere on it.

### 3. "A customer found a bag of our coffee in her cupboard. It was 4 months old." (9)
- **Why it won:** it is a real event with a person in it, and it sounds like Sam telling a story across the counter.
- **Weakness:** the longest of the three at 16 words. On video it needs a prop to look at.
- **Video**
  - SAY: A customer found a bag of our coffee in her cupboard. It was 4 months old.
  - SHOW: Mid: Sam at the bench, reaches to the back of a shelf and pulls out a dusty bag.
  - TEXT ON SCREEN: 4 months old.
- **Carousel:** `{ "layout": "hook", "title": "A 4 month old bag. *Still OK?*" }`
- **LinkedIn**
  - Line 1: A customer emailed last week about a bag of our coffee she found at the back of her cupboard.
  - Line 2: The roast date on it was 4 months old, and she wanted to know if she could still drink it.
