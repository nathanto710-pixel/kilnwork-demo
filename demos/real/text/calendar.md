# Content plan: Lowtide Coffee
2 weeks, Mon 16 Nov to Sun 29 Nov 2026.

**Goal:** More people who make coffee at home trying a bag roasted to order.

This plan says what to make and when. It does not promise any result.

## Pillars
- **Read the bag** (Teach): People cannot value freshness until they can check it for themselves.
- **Roast days** (Show): Seeing Mondays and Thursdays at the roaster makes roasted to order real.
- **Fresh, not old** (Stand for): The promise of delivery within 7 days of roasting, and the thinking behind it.
