Subject: The email about a 4 month old bag
Preview: She asked if it was still OK to drink.

I got an email last week, and one line in it got me.

A customer had found a bag of our coffee at the back of her cupboard. The roast date on it was 4 months old, and she wanted to know if she could still drink it.

She could. It's safe. It just won't taste like much, and I told her so.

Then she wrote back. She said she had never looked for a roast date before. Not on ours, not on any coffee. She didn't know it was a thing.

So this letter is about that one date: where to find it, what to do with it, and what it means when it isn't there.
