How to check a bag of coffee before you buy it.

It takes one look, and you're looking for a single date.

1. Find the roast date. Not the best before date. The day it was roasted. On our bags it's on the front, in big type. On a supermarket bag I bought last month to check, it wasn't there at all.

2. No roast date? Then you can't tell how old the coffee is. A best before date doesn't say when it was roasted. A customer told me last week she had never looked for one, so you're in good company.

3. Count back from today. I tell people to drink coffee within about a month of the roast date. That's my rule of thumb, not a law.

4. Already have an old bag at home? That same customer had one from 4 months ago and asked if she could drink it. My answer was yes, but don't expect much flavour.

That's all of it. One date, one bit of counting.

There's probably a bag in your kitchen. Go and look at it before your next cup.
