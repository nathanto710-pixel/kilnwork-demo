A customer emailed last week about a bag of our coffee she found at the back of her cupboard.

The roast date on it was 4 months old.

She wanted to know if it was still OK to drink.

I told her the truth. It's safe. It just won't taste like much.

Her reply is the bit I keep thinking about. She said she had never looked for a roast date before. On any coffee. She didn't know it was a thing.

That's the reason we print it big on the front of the bag. Not on the back.

I always say coffee is a fresh food, like bread. The day it was roasted tells you more than anything else we could print on there.

We roast to order on Mondays and Thursdays, and it's at your door within 7 days of roasting. That's the whole promise.

She ordered again, by the way.

If there's a bag in your kitchen right now, go and look. Is there a roast date on it?
