# Find the roast date

Format: short
Target: 30 seconds
Pace: 150

## Hook
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| How old is the coffee in your kitchen? | Detail: a hand takes a bag of coffee from a shelf and turns it over. Text on screen: "How old is this?" |

## Beat 1 - where to look
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| Turn the bag over. You're looking for two words. Roasted on. | Close up: finger runs down the back of the supermarket bag, reading. |

## Beat 2 - the bag with no date
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| I bought this one last month. Best before date, yes. | Detail: finger stops on the best before date. |
| Roast date? Nowhere. | Close up: the bag turned front, back, bottom. Nothing. |

## Turn
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| So I can't tell how old it is. And neither can you. [pause 1] | Mid: Sam at the bench, puts the bag down and pushes it aside. |

## Beat 3 - what to look for instead
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| Ours is on the front. | Close up: Lowtide bag held up, thumb next to the roast date. |
| My rule: drink it within about a month of that date. | Same shot, slow push in on the date. Text on screen: "Within about a month". |

## Call to action
| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| Go and look at the bag in your kitchen. | Mid: Sam to camera, points past the lens. |

## Alternative hooks
1. Instruction. SAY: "Go and get the coffee out of your cupboard." / SHOW: Mid: Sam at the bench, pointing off camera, already holding a bag.
2. Show the thing first. SAY: "This is a roast date. It's the only date on the bag I care about." / SHOW: Detail: thumb next to the roast date on the Lowtide bag.
3. A real moment. SAY: "Last month I bought this at the supermarket. Watch me look for the roast date." / SHOW: Over the shoulder: the supermarket bag in Sam's hands, being turned.

## Film it today
**Gather (5 minutes)**
- [ ] The supermarket bag (best before date, no roast date)
- [ ] One Lowtide bag with the roast date on the front
- [ ] Phone charged, lens wiped, enough storage
- [ ] Do not disturb turned on

**Set up (5 minutes)**
- [ ] Place: the bench in the roastery, facing the window, light on your face not behind you
- [ ] Phone upright at eye height, propped on a shelf or a stack of boxes
- [ ] Quiet: roaster and extractor fan off while you record
- [ ] Record 5 seconds and play it back: can you hear yourself clearly?

**Talking shots (film in this order)**
- [ ] Mid, phone on the shelf: the Turn line, then the Call to action line
- [ ] Close up, phone in your other hand: Beat 1, both Beat 2 lines, both Beat 3 lines
- [ ] Say each line twice. Leave a second of silence before and after.

**B-roll (10 seconds each)**
- [ ] A hand taking a bag from a shelf and turning it over (for the hook)
- [ ] Finger on the best before date
- [ ] The supermarket bag turned front, back and bottom
- [ ] Thumb beside the roast date on the Lowtide bag, phone moving slowly closer

**Before you pack up**
- [ ] Watch every clip once with sound
- [ ] One extra take of the hook, with more energy than feels natural

## Notes
- Text on screen is added in the edit: "How old is this?" and "Within about a month".
- Caption idea, same action as the video: "Go and look at the bag in your kitchen. Roast date, or only a best before?"
- Facts used: the supermarket bag bought last month, best before but no roast date; the roast date on the front of the Lowtide bag; the person's rule of thumb of about a month.
